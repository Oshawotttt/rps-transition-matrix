Sign-in screen, three drafts

Each draft replaces the Host / Join as guest screen. The robot is the host (this laptop runs the bot on the projector); the human is the guest (the friend's own device). Click either character. Use the controls above each frame for the checking and refused states, and to see it at phone width, where most guests open it.

1The room plan

The screen as a map of the room. The robot sits at the presenter's desk under the projector; the human sits in the corner, joined by the room wifi. Picking a role means picking where this screen is. It also explains, without a paragraph, why only the projector laptop can host.

Rock, paper, scissors against the bot

Where is this screen? The host runs the match on the projector. The guest plays the bot from a corner of the room.

Hosting works only on the laptop running the server. On this laptop, choose Join as guest.

2Two cards on the table

The roles as the game's own cards. The same face-up cards the projector deals, drawn large, each with its player standing at the table. A hovered card straightens and lifts like a card being picked up. When hosting is refused, the robot's card turns face down and says why on its back.

3Stand up to play

The projector's scene, waiting for its players. Both characters sit low behind the table. Point at a side and that player stands up while the other sits back down; click to take that side. Each half of the screen is the button, so it is hard to miss on a phone.
